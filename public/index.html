Security & Encryption

Cryptographic standards protecting FLUXDROPE transfers.

Seven-Attempt Lockout Policy

Enforced atomically on the Cloudflare Durable Object session. Allows up to 6 unsuccessful password attempts. On the 7th unsuccessful attempt, verification is locked for 10 minutes.

End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

Files encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Keys derived from password using PBKDF2 (100,000 rounds) with unique 96-bit initialization vector per chunk.

Mandatory Branding Policy

Every downloaded file preserves original bytes while adopting: OriginalFileName_FLUXDROPE_Created_By_Shiv_Yogi.Extension

Help and Troubleshooting

Frequently encountered WebRTC connection questions.

Transfer hangs at "Negotiating WebRTC"?

WebRTC requires symmetric or STUN-traversable networks. Ensure neither device is using a restrictive corporate VPN or firewall blocking UDP ports.

Where do downloaded files save?

Files are downloaded directly into your browser's default Downloads folder with the mandatory Shiv Yogi branding name format.

Can I preview files before downloading?

Yes! FLUXDROPE includes universal viewers for Video, Audio (with dynamic waveform), Images (with zoom & watermark copy), PDFs, and Documents (CSV, JSON, Markdown).
